## 1. Corporate facts: legal entity, founding and launch dates, ownership, leadership, HQ

### Takeaway
yoxi is the Hotai Group's taxi ride-dispatch brand (「乘車派遣服務」). It launched officially on **2020-11-19**. The brand, website and passenger app are run by **和泰聯網股份有限公司 (Hotai Connected Co., Ltd.)**, a 和泰汽車 subsidiary. The fleet and dispatch company, **和泰移動服務股份有限公司**, was set up on 2020-04-17. Its board seats are held by representatives of 和泰聯網, plus one supervisor representing 和潤企業. I found **no evidence of any equity stake held by JapanTaxi, Mobility Technologies, GO Inc. or Uber**. GO (since 2025-07) and Uber (since 2024-02) are commercial partners only. The current public face is **邱威凱, General Manager of 和泰聯網 (2026)**.

## 2. Operating model: platform vs. operator, driver contracting, vehicles, service areas

### Takeaway
yoxi is a **dispatch platform plus a branded fleet (「車隊」)** that licensed, mostly independent taxi drivers join (「入隊」). Drivers own or lease their cars and affiliate (靠行) with a 車行, including two Hotai-contracted 車行 in northern Taiwan. yoxi dispatches both **metered 小黃** and **app-priced 多元** rides, all in TOYOTA or Lexus cars under five years old. Its revenue from drivers is a dispatch fee, which ranges from 「0元起」 on regional plans to 10% on 多元 and 尊榮 plans. It recruits drivers by bundling Hotai car loans, maintenance and insurance perks. Current coverage is **13 counties/cities for 小黃** (苗栗 only partly) and **9 for 多元**, plus **Japan (46 prefectures) via GO**.

**Market context**

### Inferences
- In practice, yoxi is **both a platform and a branded fleet**. Legally it appears to be registered as a **dispatch business**, with vehicles and licences held by drivers and their 車行 rather than by yoxi.
- yoxi says it filed its cancellation fee in its 「營運計劃書」 with the regulator ([聯合報 2025-10-04](https://udn.com/news/story/7266/9049760)). That implies it operates under an operating plan approved by the regulator.
- Hotai's distinctive flywheel is the **driver lifecycle**. TOYOTA already dominates taxi vehicles, so loans, maintenance, insurance and leasing perks recruit drivers, and the dispatch fee can be kept low. This shapes the economics of any new feature: services that help riders could also be pitched as drivers' income tools.

## 3. Scale: fleet, drivers, members, downloads, rides, revenue, market share

### Takeaway
yoxi has **never publicly disclosed revenue, profit, MAU/DAU, app-open frequency or market share**. The latest company-supplied figures (2026-07) are:
- More than 2 million app downloads.
- More than 10,000 partner drivers.
- More than 60,000 app reviews averaging 4.8.
- More than 650 B2B clients, with corporate trips up 23% YoY.

From 2024-01 to 2025-09, "active members" was stuck at "over 1 million". By disclosed size, yoxi is clearly behind 台灣大車隊 (29,000 vehicles, 7.2 million users) and LINE GO (15,000+ vehicles, 5.17 million members). As of 2023-09 it was **not yet profitable**, and management expected break-even at about 10,000 drivers.

### Inferences
- **Activation headroom.** There are more than 2 million downloads but only about 1 million "active members" (the last stated figure). The group has about 4 million members. A large share of downloaders and Hotai members are therefore not regular riders. This fits the hackathon's low-open-frequency framing.
- **Plateau.** The same "百萬 active members" figure was repeated from 2024-01 through 2025-09, and the 2026 PR switched to a downloads metric. That suggests user growth has slowed. This is an inference, not a disclosed trend.
- **Profitability.** The 2023 break-even condition (about 10,000 drivers) is now claimed to be met (2026-07), but no profitability has been disclosed. I cannot conclude anything about profitability.
- **Points.** Points-offset rides exceed 1.2 million against a cumulative yoxi+iRent base of 40 million trips. 和泰Points are a proven lever on yoxi (about 3% of combined trips, rough order of magnitude).

### Gaps
- yoxi-only revenue, profit or loss, MAU/DAU, app-open frequency, rides per user, and yoxi-only ride volume (the 40 million figure includes iRent).

## 4. Product features: everything yoxi offers, especially non-ride and AI

### Takeaway
For a taxi app, yoxi's feature set is broad: ride types, booking channels, payments, safety, accessibility, corporate billing and Japan. But **almost every non-ride mechanic exists to trigger the next ride**:
- 天天領 daily coupon draw.
- 好康任務 missions.
- 和泰Points 福袋 lucky bags.
- Partner promo codes.
- The in-car ad tablet.

AI work is concentrated on **accessibility and inclusion**: AI house-number photo recognition (「Snap to Go」, live), real-time translation, and accessible pickup points. A voice booking feature (「Say to Go」) and a community-help feature (「Connect to Go」) are reported in an unverified 2026-08 法說會 summary. I found **no** in-app lifestyle feed, check-in streak, game, membership tier, subscription, ride pass, LINE bot or AI chatbot.

## 5. Recent news and strategy 2023–2026 (positioning, partnerships, executive statements, problems)

### Takeaway
Strategy has moved through three phases:
1. **2020–2023: scale-up inside Hotai's MaaS "One ID" ecosystem.** The stated aim was to become Taiwan's largest ride-hailing platform. Losses were accepted.
2. **2024–2025: more supply and new monetization.** Uber dual dispatch, THSR stations, driver fee waivers; cancellation fees and in-car ad tablets.
3. **2025–2026: differentiation through AI for inclusion** ("Mobility for All", senior features, the Toyota Mobility Foundation (TMF) partnership), **product breadth** (6-seat, any-vehicle mode, Japan GO), and **early lifestyle positioning** (「你的城市探索夥伴」, art and music partnerships, the in-car media screen).

Hotai says the next step for MaaS is to extend into **broader lifestyle consumption**. The challenge's exact phrase 「城市移動與生活的夥伴」 does not appear in any public source I reviewed. The closest public wording is 「你的城市探索夥伴」 (2026-09).
